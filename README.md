# Degrees of 2d-ity
This project bears no formal association is Degrees of Lewdity nor its team, as of such content is liable to change upon request of the original creators
This is an opensource rpgmaker style 'mod' of Degrees of Lewdity(DoL) by Vrelnir and associates. As of such a lot of the art, story, world, characters, etc are from DoL and credit goes to the respective team members for their work.
A full credits list is in the making, but if something is not listed as made by the people working on this assume its from DoL.
